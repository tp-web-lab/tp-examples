The cat and swan arrangements are adapted from Wiebke Köpp, TangramGenerator, Code/exampleTangrams.js.
Source: https://github.com/Wiebke/TangramGenerator/blob/master/Code/exampleTangrams.js
Coordinates normalized to the tp-tangram pieces and recolored.
The airplane and house are original arrangements for this course.
The balancing figure (A2) and runner (C3) follow the placements selected from Lulu la taupe:
https://lululataupe.com/imprimerie/jeux-d-observation/tangrams/586-tangram-modeles-personnages-couleur
Their vector geometry was reconstructed with the exact tp-tangram pieces.
