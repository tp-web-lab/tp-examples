# Expression
The [TOEIC speaking and Writing tests](https://www.etsglobal.org/fr/en/test-type-family/toeic-speaking-and-writing-tests) measure oral and written English language skills for beginner to advanced levels, in a professional context. They determine a non-native English speaker's ability to communicate effectively in English with colleagues and clients.

Speaking test
: Test-takers are given specific instructions, including the time allowed to prepare and present their answers. Test-takers record their answers to questions that are designed to measure various aspects of their speaking ability (11 questions in 20 minutes).

Writing test
: Test takers are given specific instructions, including a given time limit for their answers. Test takers write answers to questions that are designed to measure different aspects of their writing ability (8 questions in 60 minutes).
