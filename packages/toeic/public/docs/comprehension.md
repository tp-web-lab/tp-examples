# Comprehension

The [TOEIC listening and reading tests](https://www.etsglobal.org/fr/en/test-type-family/toeic-listening-and-reading-test) measure written and oral comprehension skills in English, from beginner to advanced levels, in a professional context. It determines non-native English speakers' ability to communicate in English in the workplace.

[Listening Comprehension](comprehension/listening.md)
: Test takers listen to a series of questions and short conversations recorded in English, then answer questions based on what they have heard (100 questions in 45 minutes). 

[Reading comprehension](comprehension/reading.md)
: Test takers read a variety of written documents relating to everyday and professional life, then answer questions based on what they have read, at their own pace (100 questions in 75 minutes).
