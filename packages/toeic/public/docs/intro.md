# Workplace English
::: tp-toc {position="end" expand-all open brand}
:::

This interactive document illustrates `tp-components` through original workplace English activities inspired by <tp-ref href="%TOEIC">TOEIC</tp-ref> task formats. It is an independent, shortened learning resource, not an official exam or a predictor of TOEIC scores.

## Your learning route

Comprehension
: 
  - [Listening](#/listening.md): 8 questions with recordings and transcripts.
  - [Reading](#/reading.md): 8 questions about workplace documents.

Expression
: 
  - [Speaking](#/speaking.md): 5 tasks with a local audio recorder.
  - [Writing](#/writing.md): 3 tasks with editable responses.

## Sources

The exercises, models, scripts and illustration are original. The audio uses synthetic voices. The task families follow the official [Listening & Reading samples](https://www.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pdfs/toeic/toeic-listening-reading-sample-test.pdf) and [Speaking & Writing samples](https://www.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pdfs/toeic/toeic-speaking-writing-sample-tests.pdf).

::: tp-glossary {ref="TOEIC" title="Test of English for International Communication"}
Test of English for International Communication : https://www.eu.ets.org/toeic/about.html

The TOEIC is a standardised qualification that assesses the level of English proficiency of non-native speakers in a professional or workplace context.

TOEIC is a registered trademark of ETS (Educational Testing Service).
:::

::: tp-glossary {ref="TOEFL" title="Test of English as a Foreign Language"}
Test of English as a Foreign Language : https://www.eu.ets.org/toefl.html

The TOEFL is a standardised test that assesses the level of proficiency in academic English of non-native speakers.

TOEFL is a registered trademark of ETS (Educational Testing Service).
:::

::: tp-glossary {ref="ETS" title="Educational Testing Service"}
Educational Testing Service : https://www.eu.ets.org

ETS is a private, non-profit organisation founded in 1947 in the United States, specialising in the design and administration of educational assessment and certification tests.

It is the official body responsible for developing and administering the TOEIC and TOEFL.
ETS develops the exam questions, marking schemes and marking algorithms for its exams worldwide.
:::
